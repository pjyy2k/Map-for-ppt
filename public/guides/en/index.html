Maps for PowerPoint — guides and examples

Map for PPT is a browser tool for presentation maps. Choose places, style the map, then download. No installation or account is required.

Place pins with a click, then drag to move them or leave a curved route.

Download transparent PNG, SVG and editable PowerPoint maps

Choose the right file format, resolution and aspect ratio for your slide.
