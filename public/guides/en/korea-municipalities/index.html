Color Korean municipalities across different provinces

Select Korean cities, counties and districts together without entering each province first.

  1. Choose Korea map and keep the view on all of Korea.
  2. Choose Municipalities as the selection level. Search the nationwide list without opening a province first.
  3. Select, for example, Chuncheon in Gangwon and Jeongeup in Jeonbuk. Municipalities from different provinces can be selected together.
  4. In Style, open Individual region styles to change each city's color, then download PNG, SVG or PPTX.

Frequently asked questions

Are neighborhood and legal village boundaries available?

Administrative neighborhoods can be explored from municipalities. Korean detail uses administrative boundaries dated 2025-01-01; legal ri village boundaries are not included.
