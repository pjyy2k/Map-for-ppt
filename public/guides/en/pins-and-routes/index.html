Add map pins and curved dotted flight routes

Place pins with a click, then drag to move them or leave a curved route.

  1. In Style, click the map to add a pin. If a pin is selected, click an empty spot to deselect it.
  2. Drag a pin to its destination. On release, choose Move only or Leave a dotted route.
  3. A route connects a hollow origin circle to the destination pin with a curve. Set a route color that contrasts with the map.
  4. On desktop, right-click either the destination pin or its origin circle to request deletion. On mobile, select the pin and use Delete. Confirming removes the pin and its route.

Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel a drag?

Choose Cancel in the drop dialog to restore the pin and its previous route.
