Guides

Download transparent PNG, SVG and editable PowerPoint maps

Choose the right file format, resolution and aspect ratio for your slide.

  1. In Download, choose a 16:9, 4:3 or 1:1 canvas.
  2. Enable Transparent background for a PNG that lets the slide background show through. Choose 1920, 3840 or 7680 px.
  3. Use SVG for scalable vector output. You can also download individual regions or a ZIP of selected region SVGs.
  4. Choose PPTX to edit regions and pins as separate PowerPoint shapes. Save a JSON project from the Project menu to continue editing later.

Frequently asked questions

Can I recolor separate regions inside a PNG in PowerPoint?

A PNG is one image. Download editable PPTX if you need separate region and pin shapes.
