Color countries and continents on a world map for PowerPoint

Show markets, travel destinations and supply chain locations on a presentation map.

  1. Choose World map in the first step. View area controls the visible extent; Selection level controls which geographic units you can highlight.
  2. Choose continents or countries, then check the places you need. To select states or provinces, first choose a single country in View area. Municipalities are unavailable in the world view.
  3. In Style, choose fill, flag or border. Open Individual region styles to give different places different colors.
  4. Download PNG and insert it into PowerPoint using Insert → Pictures, or choose an editable PPTX.

Frequently asked questions

Does the editor load states for the whole world at once?

No. It loads state/province boundaries for the single country chosen in View area.
